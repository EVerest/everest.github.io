###########
ISO15118_ev
###########

| This interface defines a simple ISO15118 ev.


*********
Variables
*********
| **v2g_messages**: *object*  (:ref:`iso15118-V2gMessages <iso15118-V2gMessages>`)
|  The id of each V2G response the EV accepted, for every protocol. Requests the EV sends are not reported, and the JSON and EXI fields are never populated. The CP B transition after PowerDelivery(Stop) is signaled by power_delivery_stopped, not by this variable.
| **power_delivery_stopped**: *null* 
|  The SECC confirmed PowerDelivery(Stop); the EV may open its contactor and set CP B.
| **welding_detection_failed**: *object*  (:ref:`iso15118-WeldingDetectionFailure <iso15118-WeldingDetectionFailure>`)
|  DC welding detection reached its response or time limit without a safe EVSE present voltage, either because the reported voltage stayed unsafe or because no response was accepted, so a welded contactor cannot be ruled out. The EV terminates the session.
| **v2g_session_finished**: *null* 
|  The v2g session between the charger and the car is finished
| **ev_power_ready**: *boolean* 
|  The car is ready for power (HLC)
| **ac_evse_max_current**: *number* 
|  EVSE max current per phase
| **ac_evse_target_power**: *object*  (:ref:`iso15118-AcTargetPower <iso15118-AcTargetPower>`)
|  Evse AC max target power
| **dc_power_on**: *null* 
|  The ev wants to close the dc contactors
| **stop_from_charger**: *null* 
|  The charger wants to stop the charging process
| **pause_from_charger**: *null* 
|  The charger wants to pause the session (only d20)
| **dc_evse_present_current**: *number* 
|  EVSE-delivered present current during DC charging (A)
| **dc_evse_present_voltage**: *number* 
|  EVSE-delivered present voltage during DC charging (V)
| **der_negotiated_functions**: *object*  (:ref:`iso15118-DerNegotiatedFunctions <iso15118-DerNegotiatedFunctions>`)
|  The DER control functions negotiated for the session, for either DER annex: the demand of the selected parameter set for AC_DER_IEC, the enabled modes the EV echoes for AC_DER_SAE. AC_DER_IEC publishes once, from ServiceDetail; AC_DER_SAE once per ChargeParameterDiscovery round and in the charge loop whenever the set changes. The functions are in iec_functions for AC_DER_IEC and in sae_functions for AC_DER_SAE.
| **der_control_received**: *object*  (:ref:`iso15118-DerControlReceived <iso15118-DerControlReceived>`)
|  Summary of each DER control block the SECC sends, for either DER annex, with one exception: AC_DER_IEC charge-loop blocks of the Scheduled control mode are not published. AC_DER_IEC Dynamic charge-loop blocks are published for the first of a session and then only when their setpoints change; ChargeParameterDiscovery blocks of either annex always are.

********
Commands
********
| **start_charging**: *boolean*
|  Start the ev charging process
|  **EnergyTransferMode**: *string* <required> (:ref:`iso15118-EnergyTransferMode <iso15118-EnergyTransferMode>`)
|   Selected energy transfer mode for charging that is requested by the EVCC
|  **SelectedPaymentOption**: *object* <required> (:ref:`iso15118-SelectedPaymentOption <iso15118-SelectedPaymentOption>`)
|   Selected payment option
|  **DepartureTime**: *number* <required>
|   Seconds from this call until the EVCC wants to leave the charging station. 0 states no departure time. Must be finite and not negative.
|  **EAmount**: *number* <required>
|   The amount of energy that the EVCC wants to charge (in kWh). 0 states no amount. Must be finite and not negative.
|  **Result**: *boolean* 
|   Returns true if the evcc simulation started
| **stop_charging**: *void*
|  Stop the ev charging communication process
| **pause_charging**: *void*
|  Pause the ev charging communication process
| **abort_charging**: *void*
|  Terminate the V2G session immediately, without the SessionStop exchange. CP state E/F stops the session ([V2G2-737]); the connection is then torn down ([V2G2-728], [V2G2-025]).
| **cp_state_changed**: *void*
|  Control pilot state the vehicle applies. Gates the EVCC CP checks, e.g. C/D before CableCheckReq ([V2G2-912]). E or F during a session terminates it without SessionStop ([V2G2-737]).
|  **cp_state**: *string* <required> (:ref:`iso15118-CpState <iso15118-CpState>`)
|   The CP state the vehicle has applied
| **set_fault**: *void*
|  TODO_SL: Set the different ev faults to communicate these errors to the charging station
| **set_dc_params**: *void*
|  Set the target parameters for a dc charging process. They are vehicle limits that stay for later sessions until the next call; a field left out keeps its previous value.
|  **EvParameters**: *object* <required> (:ref:`iso15118-DcEvParameters <iso15118-DcEvParameters>`)
|   Target parameters for dc charging
| **set_bpt_dc_params**: *void*
|  Set the bpt parameters for dc charging. They are vehicle limits, like set_dc_params: they apply to a running bidirectional DC session and stay for later ones until the next call.
|  **EvBPTParameters**: *object* <required> (:ref:`iso15118-DcEvBPTParameters <iso15118-DcEvBPTParameters>`)
|   BPT parameters for dc charging
| **enable_sae_j2847_v2g_v2h**: *void*
|  Enable the SAE J2847 2 V2H V2G
| **update_soc**: *void*
|  Updating the EV SoC state
|  **SoC**: *number* <required>
|   The actual State of Charge from the EV
| **update_present_values**: *void*
|  Updating the values the EV measures. Separate from update_soc because electrical measurements and SoC have different natural update rates.
|  **PresentValues**: *object* <required> (:ref:`iso15118-EvPresentValues <iso15118-EvPresentValues>`)
|   The values the EV currently measures
